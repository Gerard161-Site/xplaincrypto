## Tokenomics and Distribution

Tokenomics and Distribution

ONDO has a total maximum token supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens. The circulating supply currently stands at 450,000,000 tokens. The distribution of these tokens is as follows:

Team: 20% of the total token supply is allocated to the ONDO team. This allocation is crucial for the ongoing development and success of the project. The team's stake ensures that they have a vested interest in the long-term growth of the platform.

Treasury: 15% of the total token supply is reserved for the project's treasury. This allocation serves as a reserve fund that can be used for future development, partnerships, and other strategic initiatives. Having a treasury ensures the project's sustainability and ability to adapt to market conditions.

Community Rewards: 25% of the total token supply is designated for community rewards. This allocation is aimed at incentivizing and rewarding active community members who contribute to the growth and success of the ONDO ecosystem. Community rewards play a crucial role in fostering engagement and participation within the community.

Investors: 15% of the total token supply is set aside for investors. This allocation serves as a reward for early backers of the project and provides them with a stake in its success. Investor tokens can also be used to attract strategic partners and stakeholders to the ONDO platform.

Liquidity Provision: 10% of the total token supply is allocated for liquidity provision. This allocation is essential for maintaining a healthy and liquid market for the ONDO token. By providing liquidity, the project ensures that there are enough tokens available for trading on various decentralized exchanges.

Development Fund: 15% of the total token supply is allocated to the development fund. This allocation is crucial for funding ongoing development and innovation within the ONDO ecosystem. The development fund ensures that the platform can continue to evolve and grow, offering new features and services to its users.

In conclusion, the distribution of the ONDO token is carefully structured to support the long-term sustainability and growth of the project. By allocating tokens to the team, treasury, community rewards, investors, liquidity provision, and development fund, ONDO aims to create a robust ecosystem that incentivizes participation and rewards all stakeholders involved.
